## Direction contract

THESIS: The reel is literal film. Every license is a frame on a 35mm strip and its edge code is the Stellar tx hash. It refuses the category default: a dark dashboard of stat cards and a glowing chain.

OWN-WORLD: A cool lightbox-white ground with a faint glass grid. Orange-mask acetate owns the strip and the primary action. Frame black carries body ink and frames. Edge-print yellow appears only on film and black leader. Grease-pencil red is reserved for refusal. Archivo (variable width) sets display and UI; DotGothic16 is the edge print for codes, prices and lane labels. Square corners, sprocket perforations as rhythm, no cards. Raises: light is the state, so only the frame in the gate is lit (Akari); the active frame locks onto the registration pin while the rest ride slightly off register (Ben-Day); every skip and refusal leaves a grease-pencil mark that stays (club sleeve); a total three-colour commitment, with red only for a refusal (HyperCard); film artifacts fire on exact events, never as a constant filter (16mm bloom).

STORY: Before reading, a judge sees frames pass a gate and the exclusive fog. They write the script on black leader, pay the 1 XLM fee, then watch the job page expose one frame per paid license with its real tx as the edge code, read the license sheet, and pay out the artists.
